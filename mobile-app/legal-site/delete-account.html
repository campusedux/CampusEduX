Delete Account

Version 1.0.0 · Last updated 31 August 2026

You can permanently delete your CampusEduX account and personal data at any time. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

In the app: Profile → Settings → Legal & Privacy → Delete Account, then confirm. (You can also reach it from Settings → Privacy → Delete Account.)

What deletion does

Confirming "Delete Account" runs a secure function on our server that verifies it is really you (from your signed-in session) and then deletes your data. No password is collected through a web form.

What is deleted

  • Your profile (name, email, mobile, photo, course/branch/year, optional fields).
  • Your purchases and order history, wishlist and download records.
  • Your resumes, resume-builder content and generated files, and uploaded resume files.
  • Your job applications and their stage history, saved jobs and job alerts.
  • Your practice-test attempts and progress, and saved/favourite questions.
  • Your custom-project requests and any files you uploaded for them.
  • Your wallet transaction ledger and wallet top-up orders.
  • Your reviews, notifications and notification/app preferences.
  • Your referral code; referrals you made are removed and referrals that brought you in are unlinked from you.
  • Your login credentials, so you can no longer sign in with that account.

What may be retained

  • Transaction, payment and invoice records that tax, accounting and audit laws in India require us to keep, retained for the period the law specifies and then deleted.
  • Records needed to resolve an open dispute, chargeback, fraud investigation or legal claim.
  • Aggregated or anonymised data that can no longer identify you.
  • Copies in routine encrypted backups, which are overwritten on a rolling schedule shortly after deletion.

Before you delete

  • Download any purchased project files or resumes you want to keep — access ends with your account.
  • Use or withdraw any eligible wallet balance first; unspent balance may not be recoverable after deletion.
  • Cancel an active Premium subscription if you have one.

If you cannot use the app

Email privacy@campusedux.app from your registered email address with the subject "Delete my account" and your registered mobile number. We will verify your identity and complete the deletion, usually within 3 business days. We will not ask for your password by email.

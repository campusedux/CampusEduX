Data & Permissions

Version 1.1.0 · Last updated 18 September 2026

This page explains, in simple terms, which device permissions CampusEduX uses and what account data it stores. The full detail is in the Privacy Policy.

On the website: the sections below about device permissions (Camera, Notifications, and the Android-specific parts of "Photos and files") describe the mobile app. The CampusEduX website does not request native device permissions — uploading a profile photo, resume or reference file on the website uses your browser's own file picker, and there is no camera, contacts or push-notification-permission prompt. The "Account data we store", "Resume data we store", "Activity data we store" and "Payment data" sections apply the same way on the website and in the app.

CampusEduX asks for a permission only when you use a feature that needs it, and you can decline. The app does not use your contacts, calendar, microphone, precise or background location, SMS or phone permissions.

Internet

Required. The app connects to our servers to sign you in, load content, save your work and process payments.

Photos and files

Used only when you choose to add an image or file: your profile photo, a photo on a resume, or reference images and documents for a Custom Project request; and when you upload a resume file for a job application.

The app opens your device's system picker so you select exactly which item to share. On newer Android versions no broad storage permission is needed. On older Android versions the app requests permission to your photo library the first time you tap "choose photo".

If you decline, you simply cannot attach that image or file — the rest of the app keeps working.

Camera

Not used. CampusEduX does not take photos or video and does not need camera access.

Notifications

Used to show you updates such as order and payment confirmations, job-application updates and announcements. On Android 13 and above the system asks whether to allow notifications.

You can turn notification categories on or off in Settings → Notifications, and turn all notifications off from your device settings. The current version of the app does not send marketing push notifications and does not register a push token with an external push service.

Account data we store

  • Your email, mobile number, name and a unique user ID.
  • Your profile photo (if added) and your course, branch and year.
  • Optional profile fields you may add: date of birth, gender and city.
  • Your password is handled by our authentication provider and stored only in hashed form.

Resume data we store

  • Education, skills, experience, projects and links you enter in the Resume Builder.
  • Resume files you upload and PDFs the app generates, stored in private cloud storage that only you (and support staff when needed) can access.

Activity data we store

  • Purchases and order history, wishlist, downloads and reviews.
  • Job applications and their status; saved jobs and job-alert preferences.
  • Practice-test attempts and progress; custom-project requests.
  • Wallet transactions and referral records.

Payment data

Payments are processed by Razorpay Software Private Limited. CampusEduX stores the order ID, payment ID, amount, status and item purchased. It does not store your card number, CVV, card/UPI PIN or bank credentials.

On your device

Your login session is kept in your device's secure storage; a cached copy of your profile and your app settings are stored locally so the app opens quickly. This information stays on your device and is cleared when you log out or delete the app.

Managing your data

You can edit most fields on the Profile and Edit Profile screens, change notification categories in Settings, clear the local cache in Privacy, and delete your account and data from Settings → Legal & Privacy → Delete Account. See the Privacy Policy for your full rights.

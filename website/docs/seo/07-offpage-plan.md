# Manual off-page plan

Only use real organization-owned accounts and disclose affiliation. No automated outreach or messages were sent.

- Confirm official LinkedIn, YouTube and GitHub URLs before adding sameAs. Publish tested code examples with licenses and videos showing the actual tools.
- Participate in student communities, Reddit and Quora by answering a specific question fully. Link only where useful and allowed; do not seed fake praise, votes or repeated promotional answers.
- Offer college clubs and placement cells a useful workshop or reviewed practice sheet. Ask for attribution only for a real resource or partnership; do not imply affiliation before agreement.
- Submit accurate listings to relevant educational/tool directories after checking quality and moderation. Avoid bulk directory packages, link exchanges and paid ranking links.
- Pitch a small number of original, expertise-led articles to relevant publications. Disclose sponsorship and use appropriate sponsored link treatment where relevant.
- Respond to journalist/expert requests only within real expertise. Do not invent studies, credentials, awards or statistics.
- Produce link-worthy material: reproducible SQL examples, project-scope rubric, accessible resume checklist and original student workflow demonstrations. Report methodology for any future original data.
- Create a Google Business Profile only if the business meets current eligibility criteria; a purely online website should not invent a physical location or service area. Confirm real name/address/phone before publishing NAP.

First month: confirm profiles and one demonstrable resource. Second: two relevant community contributions and a real college-club conversation. Third: review referral quality and update useful resources. These are work targets, not promised links or traffic. Track real placements/referrals manually and respect community rules.
